What We Do

Act beforethe crisis hits.

Anticipatory Action delivers targeted interventions - cash, livestock support, health services - based on hazard forecasts before a disaster fully unfolds. When triggers are met, action is immediate, pre-agreed, and funded.

Kenya's AA programme, coordinated by NDRMA and KRCS through a national Technical Working Group, has proven this approach is not only feasible - it's far more cost-effective than emergency response after the fact.

01

Forecast Triggers

Linked to KMSA, NDMA and ICPAC probability thresholds. No political delay.

02

Pre-Agreed Protocols

Every intervention defined in advance - who, what, when, and funded by what.

03

7-Pillar Framework

Full-cycle coverage from early warning to monitoring and evaluation.

Strategic Framework

Sevenpillars.

Each pillar addresses a critical component of Kenya's anticipatory action system - from generating forecasts to delivering pre-funded interventions and learning from every cycle.

01
Early Warning
Generating timely, localised and actionable hazard forecasts that drive the AA trigger system.
02
Early Action
Delivering pre-agreed, pre-funded interventions before hazards reach destructive intensity.
03
Coordination & Governance
TWG structures, SOPs, county focal points, and data-sharing protocols.
04
Research, Innovation & Learning
Cost-benefit evidence, technology, academic partnerships, and structured learning cycles.
05
Policy & Advocacy
Embedding AA in Kenya's DRM Act, county frameworks, and sector policies.
06
Financing
Forecast-based financing, contingency funds, parametric insurance, and pooled mechanisms.
07
Monitoring & Evaluation
Real-time dashboards, after-action reviews, impact evaluations and annual learning reviews.
