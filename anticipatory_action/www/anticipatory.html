Framework

Seven Strategic Pillars

Kenya's Anticipatory Action Roadmap is structured around seven guiding pillars that build a comprehensive, multi-agency system for proactive disaster risk management.

01
Early Warning

Data, triggers and thresholds — developing robust impact-based forecasting systems and harmonized trigger frameworks across agencies.

02
Early Action

Planning and operations — proactive interventions to protect vulnerable populations before disasters fully unfold, scaling reach and impact.

03
Coordination & Governance

Integrated multi-agency frameworks at national and county levels, eliminating fragmentation and ensuring complementary, efficient action.

04
Research, Innovation & Learning

Evidence-based approaches using advanced technologies, cost-benefit analysis, and international partnerships to continuously improve AA strategies.

05
Policy & Advocacy

Strategic promotion of legal frameworks, guidelines and regulations that enable proactive climate risk management at national and county levels.

06
Financing

Sustainable, country-led funding mechanisms integrated into DRM frameworks, reducing dependency on donor-driven resources for long-term resilience.

07
Monitoring & Evaluation

Knowledge management systems and cross-learning forums to document evidence, share best practices, and drive continuous improvement in AA approaches.

Why It Matters

From Reactive Response to Proactive Resilience

Kenya faces increasing climate-related disasters — from prolonged droughts affecting over 5 million people annually to devastating floods. The AA Roadmap (2024–2029) transforms how Kenya prepares for and responds to these hazards.

Pre-Arranged Funding

Dedicated financial mechanisms ensure resources are available before a disaster strikes, enabling timely and effective action.

Impact-Based Forecasting

Combining hazard forecasts with vulnerability data to predict when, where, and how communities will be affected.

Community-Centred Approach

Integrating indigenous knowledge with scientific methods and placing local communities at the heart of decision-making.

Multi-Agency Collaboration

Over 30 state and non-state partners working under a unified national framework led by NDOC and Kenya Red Cross.
