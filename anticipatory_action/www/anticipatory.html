What We Do

Acting before the crisis, not after it.

Anticipatory Action (AA) means delivering targeted interventions — cash, livestock support, health services — based on hazard forecasts before a disaster fully unfolds. When triggers are met, action is immediate, pre-agreed, and funded.

Kenya's AA programme, coordinated by NDOC and KRCS through a national Technical Working Group, has proven that this approach is not only feasible — it is far more cost-effective than emergency response after the fact.

Forecast-Based Triggers

Actions are linked to probability thresholds from KMD, NDMA, and ICPAC forecasts — removing political delay from the activation equation.

Pre-Agreed Protocols

Every intervention is defined in advance: who does what, for whom, funded by what mechanism, activated at what threshold.

Seven-Pillar Framework

From early warning to M&E, Kenya's AA programme covers the full cycle — ensuring no part of the system is left unaddressed.

Programme Impact

Measurable results, before the crisis.

Kenya's AA programme has consistently demonstrated that pre-emptive action is more humane, more equitable, and significantly cheaper than emergency response.

01

Faster than Emergency Response

From trigger to cash in hand: AA protocols have achieved 72-hour disbursement where traditional emergency response takes 3–6 weeks.

02

Proven Cost Efficiency

Every $1 invested in anticipatory action generates up to $7 in avoided humanitarian and recovery costs — evidence from Kenya's drought cycles.

03

Dignity and Agency for Communities

Early cash transfers allow households to make their own protective decisions before crisis sets in — not just receive aid after loss.
