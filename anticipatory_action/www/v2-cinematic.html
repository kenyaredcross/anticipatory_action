Kenya's arid and semi-arid lands are home to pastoralist communities most vulnerable to recurring drought and flood cycles.

What We Do

Acting before the crisis hits.

Anticipatory Action means delivering targeted interventions — cash, livestock support, health services — based on hazard forecasts before a disaster fully unfolds. When triggers are met, action is immediate, pre-agreed, and funded.

Kenya's AA programme, coordinated by NDOC and KRCS through a national Technical Working Group, has proven this approach is not only feasible — it is far more cost-effective than emergency response.

Forecast Triggers

Linked to KMD, NDMA, ICPAC probability thresholds — no political delay.

Programme Impact

Results before the crisis.

Kenya's AA programme consistently demonstrates that pre-emptive action is more humane, more equitable, and significantly cheaper than emergency response after the fact.

01

Faster Response

72-hour disbursement achieved where traditional emergency response takes 3–6 weeks.

02

Cost Efficiency

Every $1 in anticipatory action generates up to $7 in avoided costs — proven in Kenya's drought cycles.

03

Community Dignity

Early cash transfers allow households to make their own protective decisions before crisis sets in.
